What's new

Changes to the Plorea API, in the order integrators saw them, and changes to these docs. The newest entry is first. The date is the day the change was observed or announced, not a release date from Plorea.

Verified We saw this on the wire, in test or production. Stated by Plorea Plorea told us. We could not test it. Unobserved We have not tried it. Open question Unresolved, or not confirmed by Plorea.

October 2026

2026-10-20

  • The late trial-end charge is explained and fixed. Create stored trialEndsAt as sent, with its offset, and the scheduler compared that text against UTC. A minimum wait of 15 minutes after create also applied. Plorea fixed create; PATCH was already correct. The scheduler now validates the format. Stated by Plorea 2026-10-20 See Trials.
  • All dates come back in UTC, for example 2026-10-19T12:02:42.000Z. A date without an offset gets 400. A date that does not exist, such as 30 February, gets 400. Stated by Plorea 2026-10-20
  • In production, trials ending on 1 and 15 January 2027 would have been charged one hour late. Plorea corrected them. Nothing was charged wrong. Stated by Plorea 2026-10-20
  • currentPeriodEnd is null until the first charge. GET /subscriptions (the list) now returns it too. Stated by Plorea 2026-10-20
  • OpenAPI 0.14.0 replaces 0.13.0, which had a format error. Stated by Plorea 2026-10-20 The PHP SDK is hand-written and was not affected.
  • SDK: trialUntil() sends trialEndsAt in UTC with Z, the same as nextChargeAt.

2026-10-19

  • A scheduled subscription charge refunds through the API. POST /payments/refund on {subscriptionId}-{chargeId} answered refund_requested and settled 34 seconds later. GET /payments/status resolved the reference and showed subscriptionId, chargeId, refundedTotal, refundStatus and a refunds list. The lastRefund* fields were absent. Verified in test, one charge. See Field notes.
  • A refused cancel reads cancelled. A cancel on the charged charge answered cancel_requested, not 409. Adyen refused it, but the status and the charge still turned cancelled. The money stayed charged. Verified in test. Never cancel a charged subscription charge.
  • No payment.refunded webhook arrived for the subscription-charge refund. Verified in test. Reported to Plorea.
  • SDK: PaymentStatus maps subscriptionId, chargeId, refundedTotal, refundStatus, refunds and the cancel* group, and adds isSubscriptionCharge() and isCancellationRefused().

2026-10-17

  • A paid pay-by-link accepts a second payment. The link URL shows the form again after payment. A second payment was authorised, on two separate links. Each payment sent its own payment.authorised with the same reference and a different pspReference. GET /payments/status keeps the first. A refund targets the first payment, and a second full refund fails with 422, so the second payment cannot be refunded through the API. Verified in test. Store data.pspReference from every webhook. See Field notes.
  • Declined, then paid: the status and the refund point at the declined try. The status pspReference stays on the declined attempt after a good second try. An API refund was accepted for the declined PSP reference, and refundedTotal reached the full amount, so the payment that took money can no longer be refunded through the API. The decline webhook has 12 keys and no refusalReason. Verified in test. No REFUND webhook arrived in about 19 minutes. See Field notes.
  • Asked of Plorea, not delivered: block a second payment on a paid link, make the status pspReference the payment that took money, and let a refund name a pspReference. See the wishlist.
  • Docs and SDK guidance: store data.pspReference per payment. Refund a double payment, or a payment that followed a declined try, by hand. No SDK code changed.

2026-10-14

  • A moved nextChargeAt is explained. Before 2026-10-05, PATCH ignored nextChargeAt without an error. The scheduler also does not charge the same period twice: a date inside a paid period waits until the period ends. Stated by Plorea See Charge after resume.
  • PATCH returns 409 with paidUntil when the new nextChargeAt is inside a paid period. Send allowEarlyCharge: true to charge earlier anyway. Stated by Plorea Tested by Plorea in test.
  • No drift after a retry. The next period now starts at the end of the previous period. Stated by Plorea Subscriptions have a new field, currentPeriodEnd. Verified in test on 2026-10-14: it is present on active subscriptions that have a charge, where it equals nextChargeAt. It is absent on trialing and canceled subscriptions.
  • The amount can change over time on a stored card. Plorea sets no technical limit. You must tell the customer in advance, as the card schemes' rules for recurring charges require. Stated by Plorea
  • New card fields on payment methods: last4, brand, expiryMonth, expiryYear, fundingSource, walletType, isToken, tokenLast4, physicalCardLast4, physicalCardExpiryMonth and physicalCardExpiryYear. Show the physicalCard* fields: the customer knows those digits. Older methods have the fields where Adyen sent the data, so some can be null. Stated by Plorea Verified in test on 2026-10-14 on GET /payment-methods/{id}: every field is present. On a Visa test card, fundingSource, walletType, isToken and tokenLast4 were null. The old cardLast4, cardBrand and expiryDate remain. There is no list endpoint: GET /payment-methods returns 404.
  • DELETE disables the stored card at Adyen when no other active payment method or subscription uses it. A shared card stays until every method that uses it is deleted. The response has adyenTokenDisabled. Test only for now. Plorea turns it on in live later. Stated by Plorea
  • Webhooks for manual charges. POST /subscriptions/{id}/charge sends subscription.charge_succeeded and subscription.charge_failed with reason: "manual_charge". A charge that is Pending first and refused later sends charge_failed when the refusal comes. Stated by Plorea
  • Reactivate overwrites a planned date. A nextChargeAt set with PATCH on a canceled subscription does not survive reactivate. With no earlier charge, the next charge became "now". Verified in test 2026-10-14 To restart on a set date, create a new subscription with trialEndsAt. See wishlist #21.
  • SDK: Subscription reads currentPeriodEnd. PaymentMethod reads the new card fields. PaymentMethodCancellation reads adyenTokenDisabled. update()->allowEarlyCharge() sends the flag, and a 409 with paidUntil throws NextChargeInPaidPeriodException. SubscriptionChargeSucceeded and SubscriptionChargeFailed have reason and isManualCharge().

2026-10-13

  • The API key sets the environment. A plr_test_ key reaches test and a plr_live_ key reaches live. An X-Environment that does not match the key returns 403. Without the header, Plorea uses the key's environment. Lists show only the key's environment. Stated by Plorea An ID from the other environment returns 404. Stated by Plorea A test key reading a live subscription with GET /subscriptions/{id} got 404. Verified in production. This replaces the 2026-10-11 note that the test key lists live subscriptions. See Environments.
  • environment on reads. GET on subscriptions, payment methods and payments returns environment. Verified on a live subscription in production. Stated by Plorea for the rest.
  • SDK: Subscription reads environment. The SDK sends X-Environment from PLOREA_ENVIRONMENT, which defaults to test. With a live key, set it to live, or every call returns 403.

2026-10-11

  • Docs: unverified subscription behaviours are now marked. Moving nextChargeAt on an active subscription that was charged before stores the date, but no charge has ever followed it. This covers a plain PATCH and a pause then resume, with two date formats, over four scheduler runs in test (4 and 5 October). Only trialing subscriptions with a moved date were charged. Reported to Plorea on 5 October, still open. Open question Recipes that need it carry a warning. See Pause or freeze.
  • Docs: the exit from payment_failed is untested. PATCH with amount and nextChargeAt, resume (verified only from paused) and reactivate (verified only from canceled) were not tried on it. A manual reset by Plorea gave active and retryCount: 0, charged at once and moved the billing anchor. The API has no call for it. Changing amount and nextChargeAt in one update is also unverified. Unobserved See After the last retry.
  • Retry policy seen in production. From 6 to 8 October, a subscription was tried three times, about 24 hours apart, then became payment_failed, not canceled. Verified in production. Plorea has not confirmed this as a fixed policy. Open question A retry charges the amount the subscription holds when the retry runs. Stated by Plorea This replaces the earlier note that payment_failed was never observed.
  • No-trial signup. A signup without a trial was charged at once in production on 10 October. In test on 6 October, new subscriptions without a trial were not charged. The scheduler kept moving nextChargeAt 15 to 20 minutes forward. Open question Always confirm the first charge in the charge history.
  • Trial end is charged late in test. The scheduler first moved nextChargeAt 17 minutes forward, then charged about 22 minutes after the trial end. The billing day shifts by the delay. Verified in test, once. See Subscriptions.
  • The test key lists live subscriptions. GET /subscriptions has no environment filter. Check the payment method's environment before a write in an experiment. Verified Superseded on 2026-10-13: lists now show only the key's environment. Stated by Plorea See 2026-10-13.
  • Refund on a scheduled subscription charge failed through the API. For one charge in production, POST /payments/refund answered Payment not found for a scheduled charge in production, with the charge reference and with its pspReference. A cancel also fails. The first charge at signup is a scheduled charge too. Refund in the Plorea portal, then record the refund in your own system. Verified for that charge. Whether every scheduled charge behaves this way is open. Open question See Refunds and cancellations.

2026-10-10

  • Docs: 409 on POST /payments/session and 410 on GET /pay/{id} are listed. Their meaning is not confirmed. Open question See Errors and retries.
  • SDK: PaymentSession also reads Adyen's id/data. Card setup does not, because its response also carries a payment method id. Both throw when the session id is missing. PaymentLink also reads orgNr, invoiceUrl and status: "expired", and an empty value no longer hides the other name. See Objects.
  • Docs: the redirect return example now reuses the session, passes countryCode, falls back to Adyen's stored session id, and removes redirectResult from the URL only after Adyen answers. A refresh then cannot submit twice, and a failed attempt can still be retried. See Checkout.
  • Docs: with the plain npm import, pass every payment method in paymentMethodComponents. [Card] alone shows cards only. Without it the form is empty. @adyen/adyen-web/auto includes all methods. Send sessionData as JSON, because a + in a query string breaks it. See Checkout.
  • Docs: onPaymentCompleted also fires for Pending and Received. The example now shows "paid" only for Authorised, and a table lists what to show for each resultCode. See Checkout.
  • Docs: the pay page returns the customer to returnUrl only after Authorised, and ignores a returnUrl or invoice_url that is not http(s). The 409 advice is now "check the status first". See The return URL.
  • Docs: Visa, Mastercard and American Express test cards, each with a 3-D Secure 2 card for the challenge. See Testing.
  • Docs: the site is published. It covers the API as of this date.

2026-10-09

  • Production charge incident, fixed. From 1 to 9 October, every scheduled production charge failed with "PaymentDetail not found". Plorea's scheduler had charged production subscriptions against Adyen's test environment. Plorea fixed it on 9 October, and the retried charges came back authorised. Verified It was an incident, not a card decline. Stated by Plorea
  • Webhook routing. Production subscription.* deliveries went to the test URL with environment: "test" until 9 October. Plorea states that the routing is fixed. Open question until we see it. Do not filter on environment, and keep polling. See Webhooks.
  • Charge history order. GET /subscriptions/{id}/charges is not reliably newest first. Sort by createdAt. Verified
  • Refund on an authorised payment. A partial and a full refund both stayed refunds, not cancels. refund_requested means accepted, not settled. Verified in test.
  • Delete a stored card. The delete does not deactivate the card at Adyen today. Stated by Plorea
